26-week practical course

Learn security by building
the systems it protects.

A guided path from your first Linux VM to an investigated incident and a secured cloud workload. Start with the lab handbook, then complete the weeks in order.

6 months6–12 hours / week16 GB laptop pathEnglish
Read the lab handbookBegin Week 01
Outcome: Build, configure, test, troubleshoot, and explain a small security environment across operating systems, networks, identity, applications, monitoring, recovery, and cloud. This is a foundation for junior security work—not a promise of mastery, certification, or employment.

Your learning rhythm

The core path is 156 planned hours: 26 weeks × 6 hours. At 9 hours weekly you have 234 hours; at 12, 312. The additional time is for repetition, deeper investigation, and optional exercises. Installation time varies. Keep the order and extend a difficult week when needed; do not substitute screenshots of commands for understanding.

New: Elastic, Splunk & Security Onion

Six guided extensions add 36 planned hours of pipeline engineering, search, detection testing, packet investigation, and platform comparison. Includes a downloadable synthetic case and a staged 16 GB laptop plan. Open the extension track and choose a schedule →
